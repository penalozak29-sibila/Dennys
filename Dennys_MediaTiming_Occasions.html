OCCASION 03 7 PM – 9 PM · Mon – Thu

Post-Work Dinner

DATA PROOF-POINTS
13.15% eating density at 7 PM
Zillennial eating peak
7 PM is the Zillennial eating apex of the entire evening. It is the largest single dinner data point in their 24-hour cycle.
12.6% eating density at 7 PM
MC Parent eating peak (post-work)
MC Parents mirror Zillennials — their dinner eating curve peaks at 7 PM, after work density drops below 7% at 6 PM (down from 13.2% at 5 PM).
6.8% working density at 7 PM
MC Parent work drop-off
Work pressure drops 6.4pts in a single hour (13.2% → 6.8% from 5 PM to 7 PM). This is the clearest 'transition to leisure' signal in the data.
$81K – $88K
Weekday dinner volume (7 PM, Tue–Thu)
Volume is soft relative to what audience eating curves suggest it should be — this is exactly the gap the Dinner Disconnect insight identified.
AUDIENCE SIGNALS
  • The traditional 5 PM–7 PM dinner window is a mirage — both audiences are still working during this time (13–27% working density), meaning brands targeting 'family dinner at 5–6 PM' are messaging into the wrong moment
  • 7 PM–9 PM is when both segments are genuinely available: work dropped, socializing building (10.4% for Zillennials), eating active
  • 10.4% Zillennial socializing at 7 PM suggests group/couple dining — the occasion is social, not solitary; creative should reflect the company, not the food
MEDIA WINDOWS
Channel Window Rationale
CTV / Streaming 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM Post-work lean-back mode begins at 6:30 PM as work curves collapse. First available high-quality video window of the day for both audiences.
Social / Instagram 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM Zillennials shift from work-adjacent scrolling to social scrolling — feed ads in this window carry much higher relevance
Search / Yelp 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM Pre-decision search window — 'dinner near me' intent peaks in the 30–60 min before the meal
OOH 5:30 PM – 7:00 PM Evening commute home — in-transit audience is mentally available to dinner decisions in a way they weren't during morning commute

Zillennial overnight socializing
The minority who are still out after midnight are disproportionately social — they're the ones keeping the night alive, making food the next logical step.

  • The Saturday-to-Sunday overnight bridge carries the highest volume of any transition — Sunday morning begins as Saturday night's overflow
